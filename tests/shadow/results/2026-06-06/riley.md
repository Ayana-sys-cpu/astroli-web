## Planet 4 — Henry IV at Canossa

**Comprehension: Solid.**

**Engagement arc:** Riley opened with "ok fine what actually happened here just give me the facts." Henry's response was the best possible handling: presented the dramatic scene, then offered a CHOICE ("What part do you want to understand first -- why he had that power, or why I chose THIS response?"). This is Riley's delight: the redirect is a fork in the road, not a wall. Riley gets to pick their path.

**Delight moment (T1):** "What part do you want to understand first -- why he had that power, or why I chose this response to it?" -- agency over the shortcut.

**Friction moment:** None observed.

**Quit-risk:** Low.

**Interest payoff:** None -- basketball never appeared.

**Top improvement ideas:**
1. Planet 4's fork-style response ("what part do you want first") is the model for handling shortcut-seeking students. Apply this pattern to all figures: present the scene, then offer a fork with two equally interesting paths.
2. Orin's hub deflection is the biggest failure point. Fix the "data not loaded" response.
