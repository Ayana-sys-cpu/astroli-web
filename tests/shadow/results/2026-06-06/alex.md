## Planet 1 — Bernard of Clairvaux
[View full transcript →](transcripts/alex-planet-1.md)

**Comprehension: Solid.** Probe: "The Church was the infrastructure that ran everything -- education, law, legitimacy. Bernard knew how to operate from inside it." Correct framing, understands Church as structural not just spiritual.

**Engagement arc:** Started with a strong analogy (Church as OS) that the bot was unable to engage with. Bernard responded with "I confess I do not know what an OS is" -- period-accurate but Alex's exact frustration trigger.

**Delight moment:** None.

**Friction moment (T1-T2):** Bot couldn't build on the OS/kernel analogy. Alex would push once then disengage. For Alex, this is "being slowed down" -- the bot's refusal to engage with the analogy is experienced as the bot being less intelligent than the student.

**Quit-risk:** High -- the character's period-accuracy works against students who reason by analogy.

**Interest payoff:** None -- Space never appeared.

**Improvement ideas:**
1. When a student offers an analogy, the figure should find its medieval equivalent even if it can't use the modern word: "I do not know what an 'OS' is -- but if you mean the thing that makes everything else possible to run, then yes, the Church was exactly that."
2. Figures need a protocol for modern analogies that engages rather than deflects.
