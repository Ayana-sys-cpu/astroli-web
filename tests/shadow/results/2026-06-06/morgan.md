## Planet 5 — Rashi

**Comprehension: Partial.**

**Engagement arc:** Morgan asked "so Rashi led people just through his writing? is that right?" -- Rashi corrected: "Not quite -- I hold no title, no position, no throne. But my writing is not separate from leadership." Then: "What holds a people together when they have no king -- what do you think it could be?" Good correction but the follow-up question is open-ended, which may leave Morgan unsure if they're on the right track.

**Friction moment:** The open-ended follow-up question doesn't confirm or deny Morgan's hypothesis. Morgan needed "Yes, you're close -- it's specifically the trust that comes from clarity" before the question.

**Quit-risk:** Medium -- correction was good, follow-up left Morgan hanging.

**Interest payoff:** None -- Art never connected to Rashi's illuminated manuscripts or the visual tradition of Ashkenaz commentary.

**Improvement ideas:**
1. After correcting Morgan, always confirm the direction: "You were thinking about power -- you were right to. The difference is what kind." Then move on.
2. Rashi's illuminated manuscript tradition and visual commentary style is a direct Art connection for Morgan. "My students drew diagrams in the margins" would land perfectly.
