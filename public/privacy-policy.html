Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 22, 2026

Astroli is an educational platform designed for K–12 students. We take the privacy of students — including minors — very seriously. We do not sell personal data, display advertising, or use data for any purpose other than providing the learning experience.

1. Who We Are

Astroli is operated by 10X Teacher Ltd ("we", "us", or "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@astroli.app.

2. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to:

  • Students who create an account and use the Astroli mobile app
  • Teachers and administrators who access the Astroli web platform

If you are a parent or guardian of a child under 13 using Astroli, please read Section 7 (Children's Privacy / COPPA) carefully.

3. What Data We Collect

Data typeWhy we collect it

We do not collect location data, contacts, microphone audio (except optionally for avatar photo capture), or any device identifiers for advertising.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and maintain your student account
  • To personalize your learning journey and content recommendations
  • To display your progress to your teacher (where applicable)
  • To communicate important account updates
  • To improve the Astroli platform based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns

We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with advertisers.

5. How We Share Your Data

We share data only as follows:

  • Your teacher / school — Your learning progress may be visible to your assigned teacher through the Astroli teacher dashboard.
  • Supabase — Our database and authentication infrastructure provider. Supabase stores your account data in secure, encrypted cloud infrastructure. Supabase Privacy Policy →
  • Google Sign-In / Apple Sign-In — Used only to verify your identity. We receive only your name and email from these services. We do not receive or store your Google or Apple passwords.
  • Google Classroom API (teachers only) — With a teacher's consent during sign-in, we request read-only access to their course list (classroom.courses.readonly) to show which of their existing Google Classroom courses can be connected to an Astroli journey. We do not access student rosters, grades, assignments, or coursework through this API, and the access token is used once at sign-in and discarded — it is never stored.
  • Legal requirements — We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not share personal data with any advertising networks, data brokers, or third-party analytics services.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by:

  • Using the "Delete My Account" option within the Astroli app (Settings → Account → Delete Account), or
  • Emailing privacy@astroli.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request"

We will permanently delete your account and personal data within 30 days of a verified deletion request.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Astroli is designed for K–12 students, which includes children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States.

  • We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the educational service
  • We do not display advertising to any users, including children
  • We do not allow children to publicly post content visible to strangers
  • We do not share children's personal data with third parties for commercial purposes
  • Parents and guardians may request to review, correct, or delete their child's data by contacting privacy@astroli.app

If Astroli is used through a school or institution, the school acts as the responsible party for obtaining any necessary parental consent under COPPA, in accordance with the "school official" exception.

8. Your Rights (GDPR / Israeli Privacy Law)

If you are located in the European Union, Israel, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data
  • Data portability (receive a copy of your data in a common format)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@astroli.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data, including encrypted data storage (Supabase), secure HTTPS transmission, and on-device iOS Secure Storage for authentication tokens. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we are committed to protecting your information.

10. Camera Permission

Astroli may request access to your camera solely for the purpose of capturing a photo for your student avatar. Photos are stored as part of your profile and are not shared with third parties beyond our storage infrastructure (Supabase). You may decline camera access and still use all other features of the app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are material, we will notify you via email or an in-app notice. Continued use of Astroli after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
